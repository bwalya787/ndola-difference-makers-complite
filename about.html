Company Background

From Humble Beginnings to a Trusted Name

Ndola Difference Makers Limited (NDM) was established in 2021 as a private Zambian-owned company. We offer general stationery, computers, computer accessories and systems, maintenance and support services, networking accessories, office machines, printing, agricultural equipment and tools, printers, copiers, toners, inks, and general building and construction services.

The major objective of the founders is to serve customers in both public and private sectors, including areas of production, trade, banks, government institutions, NGOs, and individuals. We offer complete solutions from study, design, hardware, and software supply to agricultural and industrial materials, personal protective wear, machines, and training.

In mid-2021, NDM established its office in Ndola and started with the sale of general stationery, computers, office machines, and information and telecommunication services. Since then, we have built an excellent reputation by continuously delivering products and services with responsible and proper service.
