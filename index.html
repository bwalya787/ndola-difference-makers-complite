About NDM

Building Excellence Since 2021

Ndola Difference Makers Limited is a private Zambian-owned company established in 2021. We offer a wide range of general stationery, computers, office machines, printing, agricultural equipment, and construction services at wholesale and retail.

Our objective is to serve customers in both public and private sectors — from production and trade to banks, government institutions, NGOs, and individuals. We provide complete solutions from study and design to hardware and software supply.
